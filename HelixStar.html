for Everyone

Free, High-resolution HLA typing to assess
drug hypersensitive risk.

This website is free and open to all users. No login is required, and it does not use cookies

B-Star is an easy-to-use
web server for high-resolution HLA star-allele typing from Oxford Nanopore (ONT) targeted whole-gene amplicon sequencing data.

In addition to HLA calling, B-Star highlights HLA alleles associated with drug hypersensitivity risk when they are detected, helping users quickly connect inferred HLA genotypes to clinically relevant drug-allele annotations.

B-Star is free and open to all users, with no login required, and the site does not use cookies (as shown on the landing page).
